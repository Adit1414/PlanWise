    How is priority decided?
    • Urgency counts most: the closer the date, the higher it climbs. Overdue items jump to the top.
    • Importance counts nearly as much. A weight in % lifts it further, and a big one (about 40%+) counts as maxed out.
    • Date ranges are planned for as if it could land early in the window.
    • No date? It gets a cautious middle-of-the-road urgency and a warning until you pin a date.
    • Drag or use the arrows to reorder however you like. Reset brings the smart order back.
